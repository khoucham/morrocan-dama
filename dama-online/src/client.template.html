    How Moroccan Dama is played

    8×8 board, dark squares only, 12 stones each on the first three rows.

    • Moving. A normal stone steps one square diagonally forward. Never backward, never straight.
    • Capturing. Jump an enemy stone diagonally forward onto the empty square right behind it. Normal stones cannot capture backward. Captured stones leave the board.
    • Compulsory. If you can capture you must, and you must choose a sequence that takes the greatest number of stones. If several sequences tie, pick any.
    • Multiple captures. The same stone keeps capturing while it can. You cannot switch stones midway. Click each landing square in turn.
    • Kings (Dama). A stone reaching the far row is crowned. Kings slide any distance diagonally in all four directions.
    • King captures. A king captures from a distance: it jumps one enemy stone and lands on any empty square beyond it. It cannot jump two stones in a row, or its own stones. In a multiple capture it may turn onto another diagonal, but it cannot go back the way it came along the same line.
    • Crowning ends the turn. A stone crowned during a capture stops there. The new king cannot continue capturing that turn.
    • Winning. You win when the opponent has no stones left, or has stones but no legal move.
